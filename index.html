Early Customer Value
How much an average customer spends in their first 30 / 60 / 90 / 180 days from their first order.
How to read
Early monetization curve. These figures show the average net revenue captured from a customer over their first 30, 60, 90 and 180 days as a buyer. A meaningful uplift between the 30-day and 90-day windows indicates customers are returning beyond the first purchase. To avoid distorting averages with new customers, each window only includes buyers whose first order is at least that old.
